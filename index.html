About

Hi, I'm Return.

Commercial talent and event crew, trained in the business side of film.

I work in front of the camera and behind it. Since 2023 I've appeared in commercials and campaign stills for Coca-Cola, Toyota, Samsung, realme, Yamaha and other brands.

As a marketing and distribution intern at Warner Bros. Discovery, I worked on the Thai launches of Barbie and Meg 2: The Trench. On my thesis film, The White Rabbit, I ran locations from permits to budget, and the film went on to play in cinemas.

I'm at my best when people, schedules and details all have to come together at once.

Behind the release

Warner Bros. Discovery, Thailand

Marketing & Distribution Intern

For three months I worked on two major Hollywood releases, from the planning before release, through opening week, to the wrap-up after the run.

I saw what went wrong on each project and how the team fixed it, which taught me as much as what went right.

Barbie Thai launch, Siam Paragon

In Thai cinemas
20 July 2023
Venue
Siam Paragon
My role
Launch event, KOLs

I helped plan the layout of the launch event at Siam Paragon, looked after the influencers and celebrities who came on the night, and kept the event running in order. Before that, I helped shortlist KOLs for the campaign and sat in on promotion planning meetings.

Thesis film · Bangkok University

The White Rabbit แดนฝันอันตรธาน

Location manager Suphakorn Yanprasong
In cinemas
28 May 2026
Genre
Drama, mystery, romance
Runtime
1 h 15 min · 15+

The shoot moved through a long list of places, each with its own owner and its own rules. I wrote the permit requests, worked with every owner and agency until we had approval, and kept the paperwork in order.

I also planned how many people and vehicles went to each site, and managed the location budget. The film went on to play at SF Cinema and Samyan Mitrtown, with tickets on public sale.
